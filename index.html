About RJMF Trust

Established in fond memory of Ravindra Joshi, RJMF Trust was built with a vision to serve society. What started as a small initiative has grown into a comprehensive trust dedicated to improving lives through healthcare and community welfare. Our foundation is built on compassion, dedication, and the belief that together we can make a real difference in people's lives.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality healthcare services and community support programs that empower individuals and families, building healthier, stronger, and happier communities for a better tomorrow.

👁️

Our Vision

To become a leading trust that serves society through comprehensive healthcare, social initiatives, and wellness programs, ensuring every individual has access to quality services and support.

Our Services

Hospital and Clinical Services

Comprehensive healthcare for all ages, from consultations to specialized treatments. Ensuring a healthier tomorrow with quality medical care and advanced facilities.

Yoga Garden

Yoga is ancient Indian art of living. To avail the calm and energetic atmosphere trust is instrumental in development of Bandra Reclamation Promenade. It provides the facility for yoga and other exercise.

Open Fitness Garden

A new trend in fitness, where fitness instruments were installed in open area near Bandra promenade which are being used by local residents of all ages.

Raashi Garden and Walking track

Foresting is necessary to purify the air as it reduces pollution. The plantation what we have done is as per ancient medical science of Bharat.It also has cycling track and walkways along with football court

Solar Park

Renewable energy is natural source of energy.India is encouraging solar and wind energy.Reduces carbon footprint. Thus it helps to protect environment.

Water Purification Park

Waste water which was previously going in sea is now being treated in this facility and is used for gardening in Bandra promenade area. This is an unique facility being operated which uses waste water for beautification.
