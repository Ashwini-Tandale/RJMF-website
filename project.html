Our Projects

Yoga Garden

Yoga Garden is designed to provide a calm and peaceful environment where people can practice yoga, meditation, and breathing exercises.It is located at Bandra Reclamation Promenade and helps residents experience a healthy lifestyle away from city stress.The garden is surrounded by greenery and open space, which creates an energetic and positive atmosphere.It is also a popular place for group yoga sessions, morning exercises, and cultural wellness programs.

Open Fitness Garden

The Open Fitness Garden introduces modern fitness instruments in an outdoor environment.It is especially beneficial for people who cannot afford gym memberships but still want to stay fit.Local residents of all age groups use the equipment for strength training, stretching, and cardio exercises.It promotes community fitness by encouraging group workouts and spreading awareness about healthy living.

Raashi Garden & Walking Track

Raashi Garden is a green zone created to reduce pollution and purify the air.The plantation here is inspired by ancient Indian medical science (Ayurveda), making it both useful and cultural.It has facilities like a walking track, a cycling track, and even a football court for sports lovers.The garden encourages people to adopt a routine of walking and jogging in a natural, pollution-free atmosphere.

Solar Park

Solar Park is an initiative to promote renewable energy sources like solar and wind energy.It reduces dependence on fossil fuels and helps to cut down the overall carbon footprint.The energy generated here is used to support lighting and other facilities in the promenade area.By using solar power, the park spreads awareness about clean energy and inspires people to adopt eco-friendly practices.

Waste Water Purification Plant

The plant is specially built to treat wastewater that was earlier discharged into the sea.After purification, this water is reused for gardening, irrigation, and beautification of the Bandra promenade.It is an eco-friendly step that saves water and reduces wastage, promoting sustainability.This unique initiative also helps maintain greenery in the area throughout the year without putting stress on fresh water supplies.
